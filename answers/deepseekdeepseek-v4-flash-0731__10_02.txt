**AtlasRemit RACI – Transfer Processing**

R = Responsible (executes the activity)  
A = Accountable (owns completion/decision)  
C = Consulted (provides input)  
I = Informed (receives update)

| Activity | Transaction Analyst | Regulatory Liaison | IT Support |
|---|---|---|---|
| Receive and log transfer instruction | R/A | — | — |
| Screen payment against sanctions list | R | A | — |
| Perform KYC Review on sender and beneficiary | C | R/A | — |
| Approve high-risk transaction after KYC Review | I | R/A | — |
| Release payment after approval | R/A | — | I |
| Ensure SWIFT message is sent | — | — | R/A |
| Archive case file | — | — | R/A |
| Notify Regulatory Liaison of case-file closure | — | I | R/A |
