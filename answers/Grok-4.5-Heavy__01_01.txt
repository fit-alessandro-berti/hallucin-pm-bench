**Decision points in Contoso’s process** (strictly as defined; no undefined activities or branches introduced):

1. **Triage_Agent_Validate_Eligibility** — the triage agent decides eligibility by checking the claim against the *FlightManifest* and the *WeatherReport*.

2. **Finance_Controller_PreApprove** — the finance controller decides whether to sign off, using the *EligibilityChecklist*.

3. **High-value branch decision** — if the refund amount is greater than €1 000, the case must proceed to **Senior_Auditor_Review** (which must include the *AuditTrail*); otherwise the high-value path is not taken.

4. **Gating condition for Process_Refund_Payment** — payment may begin only after **Finance_Controller_PreApprove** has succeeded **and**, for high-value claims, **Senior_Auditor_Review** has been completed (and the signed *BankTransferAuthorization* is present).

No other decision points, rejection paths, or alternative branches are described. The legacy activity “CustomerService_Agent_Decide” does not exist.

**All required documents / artefacts** (exactly those named in the process; nothing else):

- *RefundRequestForm*
- *FlightManifest*
- *WeatherReport*
- *EligibilityChecklist*
- *AuditTrail* (required for high-value claims)
- *BankTransferAuthorization* (must be signed)
- *RefundConfirmationPDF*
