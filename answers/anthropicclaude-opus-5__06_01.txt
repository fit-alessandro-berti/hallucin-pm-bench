**Notes**
- These are the only two genuine parallel (AND-split/AND-join) pairs in *Manufacturing_WorkOrder*.
- `Procure_Materials` and `Schedule_Machines` both launch immediately after `Create_Work_Order` and are synchronised at `Fabricate`.
