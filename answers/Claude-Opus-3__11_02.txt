Here is the executive memo on the claims handling process performance:

To: COO, Acme Insurance 
From: Senior Process-Performance Analyst
Date: April 15, 2025
Re: Claims Handling Performance Analysis - Q1 2025

The three worst-performing activities in the claims handling process are:

1. Request_Documents 
- Extreme wait time (150 min average), likely due to insufficient staffing or slow customer response
- High rework rate (22%), indicating frequent missing or incomplete documents  
Recommendation: Implement a document checklist and follow-up protocol to ensure complete submissions and minimize delays

2. Review_Documents
- Long and highly variable processing times (25 min average, 9 min stdev), suggesting inconsistent review practices
- Frequent SLA breaches (12.4%), driven by the long processing times
Recommendation: Standardize review criteria, automate basic checks, and monitor per-reviewer metrics to reduce variability

3. Initial_Assessment and Check_Coverage 
The data does not clearly differentiate a third-worst activity. Initial_Assessment has slightly higher SLA breaches (3.2% vs 1.5%) while Check_Coverage has more variable processing (3 min stdev vs 4 min). Neither stands out on the other metrics.
Recommendation: Investigate both with frontline interviews to uncover pain points not visible in the data.
