MEMORANDUM

TO: COO  
FROM: Senior Process-Performance Analyst  
RE: Activities with worst SLA and waiting-time performance  

Using the supplied process metrics jointly, the three worst-performing activities are Legal Approval, Risk Review, and Credit Assessment. Legal Approval averages 900 seconds against a 600-second SLA, exceeding it by 300 seconds, while incurring 600 seconds of waiting time. Risk Review averages 600 seconds against a 300-second SLA, exceeding it by 300 seconds, with 480 seconds of waiting time. Credit Assessment averages 300 seconds against a 240-second SLA, exceeding it by 60 seconds, with 200 seconds of waiting time.

Recommendations:
• For Legal Approval, reduce the 600-second average waiting time across its 465 cases by adding dedicated legal-review capacity.
• For Risk Review, cut the 480-second average waiting time across its 480 cases by reassigning staff to risk-review workloads.
• For Credit Assessment, lower the 200-second average waiting time across its 1,175 cases by increasing credit-assessment capacity.
