**Yes.**

**Shortest sequence (4 transitions):** `T1 → T2b → T4 → T5`

Marking trace:
| after | marking |
|---|---|
| — | p0=1 |
| T1 | pa=1, pb=1 |
| T2b | pd=1, pb=1 |
| T4 | pd=1, pe=1 |
| T5 | pf=1 |

T3 never fires, so T5 trivially precedes it.

**Why 4 is minimal:** T5 needs one token in pd and one in pe. The only producer of pe is T4, which needs pb; the only producer of pb is T1. So T1 and T4 are both mandatory predecessors. Tokens in pd come only from T2b or T3 (T3 additionally requires pc, itself requiring T2 or T6 — strictly longer). So the minimal support is {T1, T4, T2b} plus T5 itself = 4 firings. `T1 → T4 → T2b → T5` is the only other 4-step variant, since T2b and T4 are concurrent after T1.
