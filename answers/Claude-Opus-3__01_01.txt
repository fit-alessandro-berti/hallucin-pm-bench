Here is a summary of the decision points and required documents in Contoso Airlines' flight refund process, based solely on the provided process description:

Decision Points:
1. Triage_Agent_Validate_Eligibility - The triage agent decides if the claim is eligible based on the FlightManifest and WeatherReport. 

2. High-value refund check - If the refund amount is greater than €1000, the claim requires Senior_Auditor_Review. Otherwise, it can proceed directly to Process_Refund_Payment after Finance_Controller_PreApprove.

Required Documents:
1. RefundRequestForm - Completed by the customer to initiate the refund claim.

2. FlightManifest - Used by the triage agent to validate claim eligibility. 

3. WeatherReport - Used by the triage agent to validate claim eligibility.

4. EligibilityChecklist - Completed by the finance controller to sign off on the claim.

5. AuditTrail - Required as part of Senior_Auditor_Review for high-value refunds over €1000.

6. BankTransferAuthorization - Must be signed and present for the finance team to execute the refund payment.

7. RefundConfirmationPDF - Emailed to the customer after the refund is processed.
