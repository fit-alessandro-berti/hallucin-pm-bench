**Q1: Yes.**

P4 has two producers: T3 (which consumes P2) and T7 (fed by T1→T2→P3). Taking the T7 branch leaves the token in P2 untouched, so P5 can be produced while P2 is still marked — exactly the precondition of T5.

**Q2: Minimal sequence — T1, T2, T7, T4, T5.**

Trace: T1 marks {P1,P2}; T2 → {P2,P3}; T7 → {P2,P4}; T4 → {P2,P5}; T5 is now enabled and fires → {P6}. Five steps, and none can be dropped: each is the unique enabler of the next.

Note the side effect: T5 consumes the P2 token, so in this run T3 becomes permanently dead — the net is not free of such conflicts.
