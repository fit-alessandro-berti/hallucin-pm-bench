To:  Orion Insurance Executive Team 
From: Senior Process Mining Analyst
Subject: Top 3 Activities with Highest Waiting Times & Recommendations for Improvement

Analysis of our claims process over the last 12 months has identified the top 3 worst-performing activities by waiting time:

1. Request_Documents: 36.5h avg wait (120h 95th percentile)  
   Recommendation: Introduce automated document requests triggered immediately upon claim submission

2. Send_Closure_Letter: 12.2h avg wait (44h 95th percentile)
   Recommendation: Automatically generate closure letters as soon as a claim is approved or rejected

3. Assess_Liability: 7.8h avg wait (16.4h 95th percentile)  
   Recommendation: Implement a triage system to fast-track simple claims and focus expert attention on complex cases

By automating routine communications and intelligently prioritizing work, I believe we can cut waiting times by over 20% in these key bottlenecks and dramatically speed up claims resolution. Let's discuss these recommendations further and develop an implementation plan.
