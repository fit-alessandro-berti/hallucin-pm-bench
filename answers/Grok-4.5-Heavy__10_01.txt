**RACI Matrix – FinCo Order-to-Cash Process**

| Activity              | Account Manager (AM) | Compliance Officer (CO) | Finance Clerk (FC) | IT Support (IT) |
|-----------------------|----------------------|--------------------------|--------------------|-----------------|
| Receive Application   | A/R                  | I                        | I                  | I               |
| KYC Review            | C                    | A/R                      | I                  | I               |
| Order Vetting         | A/R                  | C                        | I                  | I               |
| Payment Clearance     | C                    | I                        | A/R                | I               |
| Record Update         | I                    | I                        | C                  | A/R             |
| Notify Customer       | A/R                  | I                        | I                  | C               |

**Key**  
- **R** = Responsible (performs the work)  
- **A** = Accountable (owns the outcome; one per activity)  
- **C** = Consulted (provides input)  
- **I** = Informed (kept aware of progress/completion)  

Assignments follow the stated process ownership and sequential hand-offs while remaining fully compliant with the preferred terminology.
