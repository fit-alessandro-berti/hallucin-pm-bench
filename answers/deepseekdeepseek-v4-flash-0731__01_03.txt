**a) Decision points**

1. **Tri-Color-Risk-Check** – Classify the applicant as **Low**, **Medium**, or **High** risk using the Cyan, Magenta, and Yellow scorecards.
2. **Step 4 conditional branch** – If risk is **Low or Medium** **and** requested amount is **≤ €250,000**, skip Manual-Underwriting-Review; otherwise, proceed to Manual-Underwriting-Review.
3. **TriVote Decision-Gate** – Pass if **at least two** of the three roles — Credit Lead, Regulatory Liaison, Sustainability Officer — approve.  
   *(The process text says “requires approvals from all three,” but then gives the decision rule “any two approvals out of three suffice”; the explicit “any two” rule governs.)*

**b) Documents required, in order of first appearance**

1. **Form 14B** — Step 1  
2. **Scorecard–Cyan** — Step 3  
3. **Scorecard–Magenta** — Step 3  
4. **Scorecard–Yellow** — Step 3  
5. **Deck Memo** — Step 5 *(only if Manual-Underwriting-Review is performed)*  
6. **Offer Sheet 77** — Step 7  
7. **Signed Offer Sheet 77** — Step 8 *(same form, once signed)*  
8. **ID Matrix Declaration** — Step 8  
9. **Loan Dossier 2025** — Step 9
