**Yes**, it is possible.

One shortest firing sequence is:

**T1, T2b, T4, T5**

(equivalently **T1, T4, T2b, T5**)

### Brief justification
- After T1 the marking is \(pa=1\), \(pb=1\).
- T2b produces a token in \(pd\) directly from \(pa\), never requiring \(pc\) or T3.
- T4 produces the token in \(pe\) from \(pb\).
- Both tokens needed by T5 are therefore present and T5 can fire, while T3 has never occurred.

No shorter sequence exists: T1 is mandatory, the two independent branches (producing \(pd\) via the shortcut and producing \(pe\)) must both be taken, and then T5 itself must fire.
