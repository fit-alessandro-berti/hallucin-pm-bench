Rationale: Reduce the due date for creating the employee record to 0 days to expedite the start of the onboarding process.

The key change is reducing the camunda:dueDate attribute on the "Create Employee Record" user task from "P1D" (1 day) to "P0D" (0 days, i.e. same day). This will require creating the new employee's record in the HR system immediately when the process starts, rather than allowing a 1 day delay. 

By initiating the employee record creation right away, it enables the subsequent steps like preparing the contract to begin sooner. Getting the administrative tasks done promptly at the beginning should help improve the overall cycle time and avoid SLA breaches downstream. The other task durations can remain unchanged.

This single targeted change to the first user task, while adhering to the constraints of not adding/removing activities or changing the sequence flow, should have a positive impact on meeting SLA timelines for employee onboarding. Of course, the specific due dates would need to be validated against the SLA definitions and further adjustments may be needed. But reducing the allowable time for the initial record creation is a good starting point to optimize the process.
