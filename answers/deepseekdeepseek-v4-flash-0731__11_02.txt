**MEMO**

**To:** COO  
**From:** Process-Performance Analyst  
**Re:** Q1-2025 Claims Handling – Worst Performers

The data clearly identify three under-performing activities:

**1. Request_Documents**  
Highest wait time (150 min) and rework rate (22%), with 5.5% SLA breaches. The queue is a major bottleneck.  
**Action:** Automate document completeness checks before submission to reduce rework and downstream wait.

**2. Review_Documents**  
Worst SLA breach rate (12.4%), longest processing time (25 min), high variability (9 min), and 5.3% rework.  
**Action:** Triage reviews by complexity and introduce standard review checklists to cut variability and SLA misses.

**3. Initial_Assessment**  
Elevated wait (18 min), processing time (12 min), rework (1.8%), and SLA breaches (3.2%) compared with other early-stage activities.  
**Action:** Implement workload balancing/auto-assignment to reduce queue time, and conduct targeted QA on reworked cases.

These three stand apart from remaining activities on wait, rework, and SLA impact.
