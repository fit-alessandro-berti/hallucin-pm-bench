**Decision points:**

- **Underwriter Alignment Gate**  
  If the two risk scores differ by **≤ 2 points**, continue. If not, escalate to the **Harmonisation Committee** for a tie-break decision, then continue.

- **Amount Threshold Decision**  
  If the amount is **< €15 000**, the system auto-approves. If the amount is **≥ €15 000**, proceed to the **Final Micro-loan Board Approval**.

- **Final Micro-loan Board Approval (MBA)**  
  The board votes **approve** or **reject**.

- Digital Application Form (ML-APP-01)  
- Proof of Address (≤ 3 months old)  
- Latest Income Statement (single pay-stub or tax slip)  
- Community Impact Statement (CIS) template  
- Dual Underwriter Score-Sheet  
- Neighbourhood Feedback Form (NFF)
