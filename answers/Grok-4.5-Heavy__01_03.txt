**a) Decision points (gates / conditional branches)**

1. After the Tri-Color-Risk-Check: if the resulting risk rating is Low or Medium **and** the requested amount is ≤ €250 000, Manual-Underwriting-Review is skipped; otherwise Manual-Underwriting-Review is performed.  
2. TriVote Decision-Gate: the application advances if any two of the three required officers (Credit Lead, Regulatory Liaison, Sustainability Officer) approve.
