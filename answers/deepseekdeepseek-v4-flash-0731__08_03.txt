Yes.

A shortest firing sequence that contains both **T5** and **T3**, with **T5 before T3**, is:

**T1, T2b, T4, T5, T6, T3**

Firing it:

1. **T1**: `p0 → pa + pb`
2. **T2b**: `pa → pd`  
   (shortcut: produces `pd` without needing `pc` or `T3`)
3. **T4**: `pb → pe`
4. **T5**: `pd + pe → pf`  
   At this point **T5 fires before T3 has fired**.
5. **T6**: `pf → pc`
6. **T3**: `pc → pd`

So the ordering **T5 before T3** is reachable.

Why it is shortest for that ordering:

- **T1** must be the first transition.
- To enable **T5** before **T3**, `pd` must come from **T2b** and `pe` from **T4**, so **T5** cannot occur before the 4th transition.
- After **T5**, `pc` can only be produced via **T6**, so **T3** cannot occur before the 6th transition.

Thus the sequence **T1, T2b, T4, T5, T6, T3** is shortest.
