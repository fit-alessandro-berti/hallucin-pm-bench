### Decision points in Contoso’s process

1. **Triage eligibility decision**  
   - The triage agent checks the submitted claim against the **FlightManifest** and the **WeatherReport**.  
   - Decision: is the claim eligible?

2. **Finance controller pre-approval decision**  
   - The finance controller signs off using the **EligibilityChecklist**.  
   - Decision: should the refund be pre-approved?

3. **High-value classification decision**  
   - Decision: is the refund amount **greater than €1,000**?  
   - If yes, the case must proceed to **Senior_Auditor_Review**, including the **AuditTrail**.  
   - If no, the high-value review is not required.

4. **Payment release condition/gate**  
   - **Process_Refund_Payment** may begin only after:  
     - **Finance_Controller_PreApprove** has succeeded, and  
     - for high-value claims, **Senior_Auditor_Review** has been completed.  
   - Additionally, a signed **BankTransferAuthorization** must be present before payment is executed.

---

### Required documents and artefacts

- **RefundRequestForm** — submitted by the passenger.
- **FlightManifest** — used by triage for eligibility validation.
- **WeatherReport** — used by triage for eligibility validation.
- **EligibilityChecklist** — used by the finance controller for pre-approval.
- **AuditTrail** — required only for high-value claims over €1,000, as part of Senior_Auditor_Review.
- **BankTransferAuthorization** — signed, required before payment execution.
- **RefundConfirmationPDF** — issued to the passenger after payment.

Note: The legacy activity **CustomerService_Agent_Decide** does **not** exist in Contoso’s process.
