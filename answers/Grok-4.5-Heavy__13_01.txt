**4. Two sampling-related reasons the monthly-frequency estimate is unreliable:**

- The fragment is a non-random, contiguous time-slice from a single day only (~5-hour window on 2025-05-01). It therefore suffers from temporal selection bias and provides no coverage of other days, weekdays/weekends, or longer-term variation within a month.
