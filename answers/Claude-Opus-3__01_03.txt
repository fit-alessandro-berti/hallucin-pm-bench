Here is a summary of the decision points and required documents in Acme Bank's small business loan approval process:

a) Decision points:
1. Step 4: If the Tri-Color-Risk-Check outcome is Low or Medium risk AND the requested loan amount is €250,000 or less, skip the Manual-Underwriting-Review in Step 5.  
2. Step 6 "TriVote" Decision Gate: Loan approval requires any two approvals out of three roles - Credit Lead, Regulatory Liaison, and Sustainability Officer.

b) Required documents, in order of first appearance:
1. Form 14B (Step 1: Receive-Application) 
2. Scorecard-Cyan, Scorecard-Magenta, Scorecard-Yellow (Step 3: Tri-Color-Risk-Check)
3. Deck Memo (Step 5: Manual-Underwriting-Review)
4. Offer Sheet 77 (Step 7: Prepare-Binding-Offer)
5. Signed Offer Sheet 77 (Step 8: Request-Applicant-Confirmation)
6. ID Matrix Declaration (Step 8: Request-Applicant-Confirmation)  
7. Loan Dossier 2025 (Step 9: Finalize-Agreement)
