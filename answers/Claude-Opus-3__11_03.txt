MEMORANDUM

To: Orion Credit COO
From: Senior Process-Performance Analyst
Date: [today's date]
Subject: Process bottlenecks and improvement recommendations 

Our analysis of Orion's credit process, based on the supplied activity data, reveals three bottlenecks that significantly exceed their target SLAs:

1. Legal Approval averages 900s throughput, 50% above its 600s SLA, with applicants waiting 600s on average. 
2. Risk Review averages 600s throughput, 100% above its 300s SLA, with applicants waiting 480s on average.
3. Credit Assessment averages 300s throughput, 25% above its 240s SLA, with applicants waiting 200s on average.

To address these bottlenecks, we recommend:

• Analyze Legal's contract templates and automate standard clauses to reduce manual review
• Adjust Risk's score thresholds to auto-approve more low-risk applications without review  
• Integrate Credit's data sources and models into a single assessment to eliminate handoffs

Implementing these data-driven improvements will bring our worst-performing activities in line with their SLAs and significantly reduce customer wait times.
