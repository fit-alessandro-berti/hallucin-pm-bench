**Yes.**

Minimal firing sequence:  
[T1, T2, T7, T4, T5]

T1 puts tokens in P1 and P2.  
The path T2 → T7 produces a token in P4 without consuming P2.  
T4 then yields P5, enabling T5 on the still-present P2 + P5.  
T3 has never fired (and cannot fire after T5).  

Thus T5 occurs while T3 remains unfired.
