Yes.

A concrete firing sequence is:

1. **T1** fires:  
   \(p0 \to p1 + p2\)  
   Marking: \(p1=1, p2=1\)

2. **T2** fires:  
   \(p1 \to p3\)  
   Marking: \(p2=1, p3=1\)

3. **T5** fires before **T3**:  
   T5 is enabled because \(p3=1\) and \(p4=0\), so the inhibitor arc from \(p4\) is satisfied.  
   Firing T5 consumes \(p3\) and produces \(p6\):  
   Marking: \(p2=1, p6=1\)

4. **T3** can fire afterward:  
   \(p2 \to p4\)  
   Marking: \(p4=1, p6=1\)

So the sequence **T1, T2, T5, T3** is valid, and T5 fires before T3.
